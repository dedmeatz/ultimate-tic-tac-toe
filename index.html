How to Play Ultimate TTT

1.

The 9x9 Setup

The board consists of 9 smaller Tic-Tac-Toe boards arranged in a giant 3x3 grid (81 total squares).

2.

Board Guidance (Core Rule)

Your move dictates where your opponent must play next!
If you play in the top-right cell of any small board, the opponent is forced to make their next move inside the top-right giant board.

3.

Claiming Small Boards

Getting 3 marks in a row inside a small board claims that entire sector with a giant chalk X or O on the master board.

4.

The Free Play Rule

If you are directed to a small board that has already been won or is completely full (Cat's game), you earn a Free Play and can mark any open square anywhere on the master board!

5.

Winning the Ultimate Game

The first player to align 3 giant claimed boards in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) wins the Ultimate Match!
